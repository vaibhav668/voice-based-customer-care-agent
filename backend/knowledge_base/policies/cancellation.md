# Cancellation Policy

Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair and transparent. Please review the details below:

## Cancellation Timeframes & Refund Eligibility

| Time of Cancellation | Refund Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| More than 24 hours before departure | 90% refund |
| Between 12 to 24 hours before departure | 50% refund |
| Between 2 to 12 hours before departure | 25% refund |
| Less than 2 hours before departure | No refund (0%) |

## Important Conditions
- Refunds will be processed back to the original payment source within 5-7 working days.
- In case of bus cancellation by the operator, a 100% refund will be provided.
- Convenience fees and service taxes charged at the time of booking are non-refundable.
