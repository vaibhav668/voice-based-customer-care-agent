# Baggage Policy

Please adhere to the following guidelines regarding luggage and personal belongings during your journey:

## Weight and Size Allowances
- **Checked Baggage**: Each passenger is allowed up to 15 kg of checked luggage free of charge.
- **Hand Baggage**: A single hand bag/laptop bag not exceeding 5 kg is allowed inside the cabin.
- **Excess Baggage**: Excess luggage will be charged at a rate of INR 50 per kg, subject to space availability in the luggage compartment.

## Restricted and Prohibited Items
- Explosives, flammables, toxic substances, and illegal drugs are strictly prohibited.
- Pets are not allowed on board any of our buses.
- Valuable items such as jewelry, cash, and high-value electronics should be carried in hand baggage. The company is not liable for loss of valuables from checked baggage.
